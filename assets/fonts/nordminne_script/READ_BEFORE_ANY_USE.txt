Nordminne Script is a bold, fast-flowing brush typeface with a sense of confident flair. Designed with an expressive stroke and rich rhythm, it captures the spontaneity of hand-lettered signage while maintaining high readability.

Use underscore _ anywhere in a word to create a swash.
Example: Flower_garden

For different swashes, use multiple underlines. 
Example: Hand____drawn

Its uppercase letters stand out with wide, sweeping gestures, while the lowercase maintains a steady balance, dancing smoothly across the baseline. The connection between characters feels natural, almost like a signature in motion. It's a style that fits perfectly on a vintage café sign or the side of a classic racing car—anywhere personality meets motion.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability. 
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers. 

Designed and produced by Mans Greback, a Swedish type designer known for his high-quality calligraphy and lettering fonts. His work blends traditional craftsmanship with modern design needs, resulting in typefaces that are both expressive and reliable.
